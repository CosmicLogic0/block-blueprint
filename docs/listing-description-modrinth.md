#### Stop guessing. Start building.

Ever tried to copy a build from a picture or a video and lost count halfway through? **Build Blueprint** puts the whole build into your world as **see-through ghost blocks**. You place the real blocks right where the ghosts are, one layer at a time, until it's done.

---

### 🔥 What it does

**👻 See it before you build it**<br>
The full build appears as ghost blocks in your world. Move it, turn it, mirror it or drop it onto the ground until it's exactly where you want it. Then press **Delete** to lock it in place.

**🧱 Build layer by layer**<br>
Start at the bottom. The mod shows one layer at a time and moves you up when you finish it. If you break a block in a layer you already finished, it takes you back to fix it.

<img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1762212/description_51bfc793-b635-455e-8a22-dc85600ab918.png" alt="" width="836">

**📋 Know exactly what you need**<br>
A live list shows every block the build needs, how many you have, and how many are left. Hover any block to see how many stacks you still need. Opened chests nearby that hold blocks you still need get a green glow.

**🎯 Never lose your place**<br>
A pulsing box shows the next block to place. Middle-click any ghost block to put that block in your hand.

**🎨 Make it yours**<br>
Click any block in the list to swap it. Change every oak piece to spruce, stone bricks to deepslate, or black sails to red, in one click. Stairs and slabs keep their direction.

**📸 Copy any build in your world/Other servers**<br>
Look at a corner of a build and press **Insert**. A box starts on that block: make it as big as the build with the arrow keys, or look at the far corner and press **Page Down** to stretch it there. Save it, share it as a code, or build it again somewhere else.

<img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2105/533/copy-a-build-png.png" alt="Copying a build with the copy box" width="738">

**⚡ Creative mode extras**<br>
In Creative mode the mod can build it for you. **Auto-build** places the whole build at once, **Build layer** places just the layer you're on, and **Undo build** takes it all back if you change your mind. On servers this needs operator rights.

**💾 It remembers**<br>
Leave the world and come back, and your blueprint is still there, right where you left it.

---

### 🎮 Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Page Up** | Open the menu |
| **Arrow keys** | Move the build (hold Shift for 5 blocks) |
| **Page Down** | Turn it |
| **Delete** | Place it and start building |
| **Home / End** | Go up or down a layer |
| **Insert** | Copy a build from your world |
| **Middle-click** | Hold the ghost block you're looking at |

Change any key in **Options → Controls**.

---

### 📦 Requirements

- **Fabric:** Minecraft 1.16.5 to 26.3 (needs Fabric Loader and Fabric API)
- **Forge:** Minecraft 1.18 to 26.3
- **NeoForge:** Minecraft 1.20.2 to 26.3
- Pick the file for your Minecraft version and loader on the **Versions** tab.
- Client-side only, so it works in single-player and on any server.
