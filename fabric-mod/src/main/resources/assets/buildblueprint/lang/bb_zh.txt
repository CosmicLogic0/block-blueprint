A build is what you want to make, shown as see-through ghost blocks in your world.	建筑就是你想建造的东西，在你的世界里显示为半透明的幽灵方块。
Open the build menu, click Saved builds, pick one and press Load into world.	打开建筑菜单，点击“已保存的建筑”，选一个，然后按“加载到世界”。
Builds come from three places:	建筑有三个来源：
- the free Block Blueprint app: make or pick a build and press Send to Minecraft	- 免费的 Block Blueprint 应用：制作或选择一个建筑，然后按 Send to Minecraft
- schematic files (.schem, .litematic, .nbt) in the BlockBlueprint folder in your user folder	- 用户文件夹中 BlockBlueprint 文件夹里的原理图文件（.schem、.litematic、.nbt）
- builds you copy from your own world (see Copy a build)	- 从你自己的世界里复制的建筑（见“复制建筑”）
Page Up	Page Up
open the build menu	打开建筑菜单
The build appears in front of you. Move it to where you want to build it.	建筑会出现在你面前。把它移到你想建造的地方。
Arrow keys	方向键
move it (hold Shift for 5 blocks)	移动（按住 Shift 一次移动 5 格）
Ctrl + Up / Down	Ctrl + 上 / 下
move it up or down	上移或下移
Page Down	Page Down
turn it	旋转
In the build menu, To ground sets it right on the ground and Mirror flips it.	在建筑菜单里，“放到地面”会把它放在地面上，“镜像”会把它翻转。
When it's in the right spot, place it. A placed build is locked, so it can't slide away while you build.	位置合适后就放置它。放置后的建筑会被锁定，建造时不会滑走。
Delete	Delete
place it (press again to unlock and move it)	放置（再按一次解锁并移动）
Placing starts layer by layer building at the bottom layer.	放置后会从最底层开始逐层建造。
Build from the bottom up. Only the layer you're on is shown, so it's easy to see what goes where.	从下往上建造。只显示你所在的那一层，很容易看出什么东西放在哪里。
The next block to place glows. When a layer is finished you move up to the next one by itself.	下一个要放的方块会发光。一层完成后会自动进入下一层。
Home / End	Home / End
go up or down a layer	上一层或下一层
Blocks you placed correctly disappear from the ghost. A red outline means a different block is in the way, or the block is the wrong way round (stairs upside down, a log on its side) - break it and place it again.	放对的方块会从幽灵中消失。红色边框表示有别的方块挡住了，或者方块方向放反了（楼梯倒置、原木横放）- 打掉重新放。
The build menu lists every block the build needs: how many you have and how many are still to place.	建筑菜单列出建筑需要的所有方块：你有多少，还要放多少。
The small list in the corner shows the same while you build. Chests you opened nearby that hold blocks you need glow green.	建造时角落里的小列表显示同样的内容。附近你打开过的、装有你需要的方块的箱子会发绿光。
Don't have a block? Click it in the list and pick any other block in the game (blocks from other mods too).	缺少某个方块？在列表中点击它，然后选择游戏中的任意其他方块（也包括其他模组的方块）。
Every block of that kind in the build changes. Don't want a block at all (snow on a roof)? Press Remove from build.	建筑里所有这种方块都会改变。完全不想要某个方块（屋顶上的雪）？按“从建筑中移除”。
Undo swap puts it back.	“撤销替换”可以恢复。
The eye next to a block hides its ghost blocks (click again to show them). Shift-click the eye to see only that block.	方块旁边的眼睛可以隐藏它的幽灵方块（再点一次显示）。按住 Shift 点眼睛只显示这个方块。
See a build you like in your world? Copy it and build it again anywhere.	在世界里看到喜欢的建筑？复制下来，在任何地方再建一次。
Insert	Insert
start a copy box on the block you look at	在你看着的方块上开始一个复制范围框
make the box bigger (Alt + arrows: smaller)	扩大范围框（Alt + 方向键：缩小）
stretch the box to the block you look at	把范围框拉伸到你看着的方块
save it (again)	保存（再按一次）
Copies go to Saved builds, and into the app too.	复制的建筑会保存到“已保存的建筑”，也会进入应用。
In Creative mode, Auto-build places the whole build for you and Build layer places one layer. Undo build takes it back.	在创造模式下，“自动建造”会帮你放好整个建筑，“建造本层”会放一层。“撤销建造”可以还原。
Middle-click	鼠标中键
hold the block of the ghost you look at	拿起你看着的幽灵方块
Settings lets you change how see-through the ghosts are, the corner list and more.	在设置里可以调整幽灵方块的透明度、角落列表等。
Every key can be changed in Settings -> Key binds.	所有按键都可以在“设置 -> 按键绑定”中更改。
Build with friends: Co-op in the build menu gives you a code for your friends. The build appears in the same spot for them, and each of you builds your own part.	和朋友一起建造：建筑菜单里的“合作”会给你一个代码发给朋友。建筑会出现在他们那边的同一个位置，每个人建自己的部分。
Something placed wrong? The build menu shows how many to fix at the top. Click it to be shown each one.	有东西放错了？建筑菜单顶部会显示要修正几处。点击它就会逐个带你去看。
Saved builds > Get free builds has 1,400+ free builds from the Minecraft community.	“已保存的建筑 > 免费建筑”里有来自 Minecraft 社区的 1400 多个免费建筑。
Settings > Extras has more helpers you can turn on: raw materials, a map of the layer you're on, and scaffold hints.	“设置 > 附加”里还有更多可以打开的辅助功能：原材料、本层地图和脚手架提示。
The mod follows your game language: English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian and Chinese.	模组会跟随游戏语言：英语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语、德语、法语、俄语和中文。
